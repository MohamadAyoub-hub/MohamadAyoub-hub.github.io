What I help businesses fix

The four problems behind unreliable reporting.

Most reporting pain isn't a dashboard problem — it's a data plumbing problem. I fix the plumbing first, then the reporting.

01

Manual reporting

Replace repetitive exports, copy/paste and spreadsheet consolidation with repeatable, scheduled workflows that run without you.

02

Scattered data

Bring together ERP, Excel/CSV, ecommerce, APIs, databases and operational sources into one structured, queryable layer.

03

Unreliable numbers

Add validation, reconciliation and structured data models before reporting — so every figure is traceable and defensible.

04

Slow reporting

Create automated refresh and reporting workflows so decision-makers get consistent information faster — not days later.

How we work together

A process built for clarity and trust.

Every engagement follows the same five phases — from the first call to a documented handover you can maintain without me.

Phase 01 — Discovery

Understand the workflow

A short call to define one workflow, current manual effort, required output and acceptance criteria. Nothing built yet — just clarity on what "done" means.

Phase 02 — Scope & proposal

Fixed-scope proposal

You receive a written proposal with deliverables, timeline, source systems and price. No ambiguity. No surprise add-ons mid-project.

Phase 03 — Build

Ingest, validate, model, report

Data lands from agreed sources, is validated and reconciled, then modelled into a structured layer. Reporting is built on top — never the other way round.

Phase 04 — Test & handover

Documented transfer

Every component is tested, documented, and handed over with a walkthrough. You own the pipeline, the code, and the knowledge.

Phase 05 — Ongoing support

Optional retainer

If you want it, monthly support covers monitoring, fixes, small updates and periodic data-quality review — all defined in the agreement.
