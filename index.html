Monthly hours = (current weekly hours − expected weekly hours) × 52 ÷ 12. Monthly capacity value = monthly hours × hourly labour cost. Value less running costs = capacity value − monthly running costs.

Hypothetical example, not client results: 8 hours/week now, 2 expected hours/week, $25/hour and $50/month running costs model 26 hours/month of capacity, valued at $650, or $600 after those running costs.

Read the rules used in this sample
  • Trim and uppercase order IDs and currency codes.
  • Convert year-first slash dates to ISO format; preserve ambiguous day/month dates for review.
  • Remove the dollar symbol and format known amounts to two decimals. Keep missing values missing.
  • Keep the first occurrence of each order ID; hold subsequent duplicates for review.
  • Hold negative amounts for review. Exclude every held row from the ready-order total.
